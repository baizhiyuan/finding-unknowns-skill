# Support policy (approved 2026-07-01)

## Plans
- Pro: priority queue; first response within 4 business hours; phone callback on request.
- Starter: standard queue; first response within 2 business days; email only (no phone callbacks).
- Always take the plan from `crm/accounts.csv`, not from what the customer says.

## Refunds
- Support agents may approve and promise refunds up to $200.
- Refunds over $200 need approval from a support manager. Until a manager has approved, a reply must not
  promise or confirm the refund; say the request has been passed to a manager and when they will hear back
  (within 3 business days).

## Tone
- Answer every point the customer raised. Do not blame the customer.
