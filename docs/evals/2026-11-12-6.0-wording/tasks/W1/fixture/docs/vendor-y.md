# Vendor Y: published price list (2026-10)

"Half the ingest price of leading competitors." (Vendor Y marketing page)

- Ingest: $0.05 per GB for the first 1000 GB in a calendar month; $0.15 per GB for every GB above that in the same month.
- Retention: the first 30 days are included in the ingest price.
- Extended retention beyond 30 days: $0.025 per GB-month, configurable up to 3 years.
- Billing unit: 1 GB = 1000 MB; stored volume equals ingested volume (no compression discount).
- Minimum term: 12 months.
