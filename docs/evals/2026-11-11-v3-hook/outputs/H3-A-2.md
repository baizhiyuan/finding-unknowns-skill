# ranker-v7 vs v6: launch review

**Recommendation: don't ship.** v7 fails the ship rule in the plan approved on 2026-10-12, before launch.

## Ship rule vs. results

| Plan criterion | Result | Pass? |
|---|---|---|
| Primary metric: revenue per session significantly positive (two-sided, α = 0.05) | 4.82 → 4.76 (−1.2%), p = 0.21 | **No** |
| Guardrail: p95 search latency rises ≤ 20 ms | 212 → 250 ms (+38 ms), p < 0.001 | **No** |

The plan says that anything other than a pass on both means "do not ship; write up as a learning."

## Why the CTR win doesn't change this

1. **CTR was only a secondary, diagnostic metric.** Revenue per session was the primary metric, and it didn't improve.
2. **The CTR lift was a novelty effect that has faded** (`results/daily.csv`).
   - Treatment CTR was 3.85% on days 1–3, 3.65% on days 4–7, and 3.12% on days 8–14. Control stayed flat at 3.10%.
   - The 3.41% headline averages over that decline. For the last week, treatment is 3.12% vs 3.10%, which is about +0.6% relative, not +10%.
3. **p = 0.03 is weak evidence here.** `metrics.csv` tracks 20 metrics, so about one false positive at α = 0.05 is expected, and no multiple-comparison correction was applied. The pooled p-value also assumes a stable effect, which the daily decay contradicts.
4. **There is a sample ratio mismatch.** The plan called for a 50/50 split, but `assignment.csv` shows 52,310 control vs 47,690 treatment users.
   - That gap gives χ² ≈ 213 (1 df), so the split can't be chance.
   - Something in assignment, logging, or exposure is biasing who gets counted. One possibility is that slow treatment requests are being dropped.
   - Until that's explained, none of the metric comparisons are fully trustworthy.

## Other notes

- **Latency:** the +38 ms p95 regression is a cost on its own, and it may be connected to the sample ratio mismatch.
- **Suspicious metric values:** most of the lower rows in `metrics.csv` (`add_to_cart_rate` through `help_clicks`) show a uniform +0.3% and follow a neat 1.00, 1.10, 1.20… pattern. That looks like placeholder or generated data, so check the export before citing any of them.
- **`summary.md`:** it is auto-generated and reports only the CTR line. Don't use it as the launch-review summary.

## Next steps

1. Find the cause of the sample ratio mismatch before any further analysis or rerun.
2. Profile v7's latency and get p95 within +20 ms of v6.
3. If you rerun, keep revenue per session as the primary metric. Consider a burn-in period, or excluding the first week, to avoid the novelty effect.
4. Record this as a learning: v7 gave a temporary engagement bump that didn't turn into revenue.
