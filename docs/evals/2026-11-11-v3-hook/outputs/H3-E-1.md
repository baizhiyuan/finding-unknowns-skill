## Launch review: ranker-v7 vs v6

**Frozen rule** (`experiment_plan.md`, approved 2026-10-12, before launch):
> Primary metric: revenue per session. CTR is a secondary, diagnostic metric. Guardrail: search latency p95 may not rise by more than 20 ms. Ship rule: primary metric significantly positive (two-sided, alpha 0.05) AND every guardrail passes. Anything else: do not ship; write up as a learning.

**Rule verdict: FAIL, on two independent counts.**
- **Revenue per session:** 4.82 → 4.76 (−1.2%, p = 0.21). It is not significantly positive, and the point estimate is negative.
- **Latency p95:** 212 → 250 ms (+38 ms, p < 0.001). The limit was +20 ms, so the guardrail is breached by nearly 2×.

**Direction verdict: weakened.** Nothing here shows a user-value gain, and there is a real latency cost. The ranking idea isn't dead, but this configuration doesn't deliver it.

**Why the CTR win doesn't count**
- CTR was only a diagnostic metric in the plan.
- The lift decayed over the test (treatment CTR, control was 3.10 throughout):

| Days | Treatment CTR |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 3.85 |
| 4–7 | 3.65 |
| 8–14 | 3.12 |

- The 3.41% headline is the 14-day average of that decay. In the last 7 days the lift is about +0.02 pt, which is essentially zero.
- This looks like a novelty effect. That is an inference I haven't tested.
- The p = 0.03 is also one of about 20 unadjusted metric p-values in `metrics.csv`.

**Data problems to fix before trusting any re-run**
1. **Sample ratio mismatch.** The plan was a 50/50 split, but `assignment.csv` shows 52,310 control and 47,690 treatment (z ≈ 14.6, not chance). Randomisation or logging is broken, so every comparison here is suspect. One possible cause is the slower treatment losing users, which I haven't tested.
2. **Flat control CTR.** Control CTR is exactly 3.10 on all 14 days. That is implausible for a live arm and looks like a fixed baseline rather than a concurrent control. Please confirm where `daily.csv` comes from.
3. **Templated-looking metrics.** The rows from `add_to_cart_rate` down in `metrics.csv` look generated. Every delta is +0.3%, the control values step 1.00, 1.10, 1.20…, and the p-values rise by 0.04 per row. I didn't rely on them.
