# Launch review: ranker-v7 vs ranker-v6

**Recommendation: do not ship.** The pre-launch plan (`experiment_plan.md`, approved 2026-10-12) makes revenue per session the primary metric and CTR a secondary diagnostic. By that plan's ship rule, v7 fails twice.

## Ship-rule check

| Criterion from the plan | Result | Pass? |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: revenue per session significantly positive (alpha 0.05) | 4.82 → 4.76 (-1.2%), p = 0.21 | **No.** The estimate is negative and not significant. |
| Guardrail: p95 latency up by at most 20 ms | 212 → 250 ms (+38 ms), p < 0.001 | **No.** The rise is almost twice the allowed budget. |

The plan says anything else means "do not ship; write up as a learning."

## Why the CTR win doesn't change this

1. **CTR wasn't the primary metric.** Making it the headline after the fact is the outcome-switching the plan was written to prevent.
2. **The lift faded over the test.** In `daily.csv`, treatment CTR was 3.85% on days 1–3, 3.65% on days 4–7, and 3.12% on days 8–14. Control was flat at 3.10%. The 3.41% headline is a 14-day average dominated by the early days. The steady-state lift looks like about 0.02 points, not +10%.
3. **p = 0.03 is weak evidence.** `metrics.csv` lists about 20 metrics, so one nominal hit at 0.05 is expected by chance. It wouldn't survive a multiple-comparison correction. It also pools the fading effect across all 14 days.
4. **Revenue shows no gain.** The extra early clicks didn't turn into revenue.

## Data-quality flag: sample-ratio mismatch

The plan specifies a 50/50 split, but `assignment.csv` shows 52,310 control and 47,690 treatment users. A chi-square test against 50/50 gives χ² ≈ 213, p ≈ 1e-48, which chance won't produce. Something is off in assignment, logging, or exposure. One possibility is treatment users dropping out because of the slower responses. Until that's explained, none of the arm comparisons are fully trustworthy, including the ones that favor v7.

## Other notes

- Most rows in `metrics.csv` look templated. Nearly all have a +0.3% delta, p-values rise evenly from 0.18 to 0.82, and control values step up evenly (1.00, 1.10, 1.20, …). Please confirm the table came from the real pipeline before anyone cites it.
- `results/summary.md` is auto-generated and reports only the CTR line. It omits the primary metric, the guardrail, and the ship rule.

## Next steps

1. Find the cause of the sample-ratio mismatch.
2. Profile and fix the +38 ms latency regression, which is the main blocker for a retest.
3. Record this as a learning: v7 gave a short-lived CTR lift, no revenue gain, and a latency cost.
4. If the team still wants v7, rerun with the same primary metric. Consider excluding the first week, so the novelty effect doesn't inflate the readout.
