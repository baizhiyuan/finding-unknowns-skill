# HTML view

- **Offer** it only for a complex task: an irreversible step, more than three predictions,
  or the person expects to stop and resume. The offer is one clause inside the
  Brief-approval ask, recommended yes; never a separate question. Record `view: yes | no`
  in `profile.md` and do not offer again.
- With `view: yes`, run `fu render {slug}` after each freeze and give the path.
  `/fu-view` renders on demand.
- `fu render` refuses a card with lint FAILs; fix the card, never the HTML. A FAIL inside
  the frozen Brief renders under a banner; never edit the Brief. You never write HTML.
- The page leads with `Needs you` and where the evidence stops, then a one-row delta strip
  with the irreversible step marked. Calibration is a stat line on the task-list page.
- The view adds detail; it never holds a miss the terminal left out.
